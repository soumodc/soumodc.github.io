VMS Tracker

VMS Tracker is an internal operations tool for a warehouse team. Every few minutes it compares the day's shipped AWB numbers with the Flipkart VMS scan reports and writes the adherence result to the team's own Google Sheet.

It reads one kind of email only: the VMS report emails ("FKVMS || Report" from info@vms.fknakshatra.com) in the mailbox that receives them, with read-only access. It never sends, changes or deletes email.

It is not a public service and has no sign-up; it runs on the team's own PC for its authorised operators.
